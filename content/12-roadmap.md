# Roadmap and Milestones

*What gets made, in what order, and how progress is judged*

This master document is the reference, and what people read is built from it: a concept note and an interactive site, which is also the pitch. [Established] (D-082, D-151) CAMPUS builds first and learns from use, so the steps below start with something people can use. [Established] (D-178; D-151) Institutional progress is tracked separately, on a seven-stage ladder where each stage needs its own evidence. [Established] (D-029; D-090)

In the author's experience as a Mechanical Engineering graduate of VSU, a thesis moves in stages, each judged by a panel before the next: a title, an outline, a final defense. [Unresolved] Passing the title defense is not graduating, and no student would claim it is; the ladder below asks the same honesty of CAMPUS. [Proposed]

[Established] (D-083; D-084; D-100; D-114; D-151; D-178)

1. **Master document.** This document, the reference for everything after it. Decisions live in its register.
2. **The network's first place, with its receptionist.** The first thing people can use: one place of the network, seeded with real public information (the Citizen's Charter, VSU's service pages, and the guide's seven sourced entries) and with made-up members labeled as made up (D-108), with a receptionist at its door that points students to what people wrote and writes no answers of its own (D-178, D-181). It needs nothing from VSU (D-014). It starts once Q-33 (compose or build) and Q-58 (its name) are answered; its code will live in a repository of its own, private until its licence and the commercial path are settled (D-179, D-184; Q-77). Real members wait for a pilot and a privacy review (D-178).
3. **One learning simulation, deferred.** A second build, a simulation for one Mechanical Engineering course once the first place exists (D-178), is deferred and may be taken up later; learning is outside this proposal for now. [Proposed] (D-190)
4. **Concept note.** A short note for the first approach to OVPSAS, built from this document (D-083) and tried first on readers the author trusts (D-095). It is printed, with a PDF of the same text, and the interactive site is its companion, reached from a QR code on the note (D-100).
5. **Interactive site.** A summary of this document that a visitor can walk around in, and the pitch (D-151). Each scene shows a picture, a title, and a short summary, with objects that open a deeper scene, more context, or a source, and a link to its chapter here. Zoomed out, the scenes sit on a simple map of the kinds of place on campus, and any scene, the map, or an audience's path can be opened directly by its address. [Established] (D-152; D-153) It is built now, with placeholders where VSU evidence is still missing, filled in as field research reports. Administration and other interested parties see it early; students see it only after they have answered the survey (D-084).
6. **Student survey site.** Built on a site of its own, so respondents don't read the proposal before answering, and kept closed until VSU's written permission is in hand (D-037, D-039, D-114).
7. **OVPSAS.** In person, with the first place, the concept note, and the interactive site, and two separate asks: permission to run the survey and the interviews, and interest in CAMPUS ([Chapter 2.4](02d-network-proposal.md); D-083, D-178).
8. **Field research.** The survey once permission is granted, the student interviews, the study of office workflows and systems, and the Facebook landscape study ([Chapter 8.1](08a-facebook-study.md)), shaped by how the builds are used and run when a VSU decision needs them (D-178). Which parts need VSU's permission or an ethics review is open (Q-51), and the protocol is published in [Chapter 8](08-evidence.md) before the survey opens (D-086).
9. **The pitch, with findings.** The interactive site presented again, to OVPSAS first, with findings in place of placeholders. A problem is shown as Established only once it clears the D-038 bars; until then it stays Proposed or Unresolved. No separate pitch slides are made. [Established] (D-151)

Hop-It's Alpha is no longer on this list: delivery is a later module of the network, taken up on its own evidence, and its PRD keeps its own sequence for then: synthetic rehearsal, individual baseline, bundled cohort, evidence report, decision. [Established] (D-098)

Field research feeds Chapters 2 and 8 and supplies the interactive site's real VSU examples. [Proposed]

## The milestone ladder

```text svg=milestone-ladder
                                       Adoption
                                  Collaboration
                             Pilot
                   Permission to prototype
              Alignment
         Interest
Recognition

Tracked apart, never a stage: research permission · ethics clearance
· access to VSU's data or systems, each asked for in writing
```

[Established] (D-029; D-090)

| Stage | What it means | Evidence that it happened |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recognition | A stakeholder understands the problem or concept | They engage with it in their own words; note whether personally or officially |
| Interest | A stakeholder sees potential relevance | A follow-up or request that comes from them |
| Alignment | Institutional stakeholders see a fit with a VSU need or direction | Stated by them, ideally in writing |
| Permission to prototype | Appropriate authority allows a bounded experiment | Written approval that names the scope |
| Pilot | Users or offices take part in structured testing under that permission | Dates, participants, and agreed measures |
| Collaboration | Responsibilities, scope, data access, or governance are formally set | A named counterpart and documented terms |
| Adoption | VSU formally decides to use or deploy it | An institutional decision |

Interest is not permission, permission is not a pilot, and a pilot is not adoption. v0.1 had an eighth stage, commercialization, which is gone because CAMPUS is not a commercial product (D-029). As of November 2026, no VSU office has reached any stage. [Established] (D-029; D-090)

## Permissions

Some steps need a permission that is not a stage of support. Each is asked for and granted on its own, in writing, and none of them moves VSU up the ladder. [Established] (D-090)

| Permission | What it allows | Who grants it, as far as known | Status, November 2026 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Research permission | Running the student survey and the interviews, with a campus ID or pass for the author if VSU requires one | Asked of OVPSAS first (D-083); the deciding office is open (Q-51) | Not yet asked |
| Ethics clearance | Research with people as participants | VSU's Research Ethics Board, through its Human Ethics Review Committee, if its review covers a study by an alumnus (E-051, Q-51) | Not yet asked |
| Access to VSU data or systems | Aggregate network figures (D-058), or a look inside VSU's existing systems | VSU ICT for network figures; otherwise each system's owner, not yet known (Q-10) | Not yet asked |

Informal advice and feedback from VSU people count as none of these. [Established] (D-090) Permission to prototype and the pilot are permissions too, and they stay on the ladder as stages of support. [Established] (D-029) Each activity waits for its own gate: the survey and the interviews for research permission, and for ethics clearance if VSU requires it; the network's first place for none, since it uses public information and made-up members (D-014, D-178); and a pilot with real members for permission to prototype. [Established] (D-090)

## Environments

Work moves through environments in order, so a prototype is never mistaken for a service: local development, controlled prototype, demonstration, research test, institutional pilot (only with authorization), and production (only after adoption). [Established] (v0.1 §12.21)

Changes are classed as minor, architectural, scope, hypothesis, institutional, or strategic, and anything above minor goes into the decision register before it appears in any document.
