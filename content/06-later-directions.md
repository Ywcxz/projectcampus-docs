# Later Directions

*Possible futures, recorded so they don't steer today's work*

> **At VSU.** In January 2025, VSU created new research centers, among them a Geospatial and GIS Research and Services Center. [Established] ([Chapter 3](03-social-network.md)) A student who wants to learn GIS still has to know where to look, and finding the person who knows, across the whole university, is one of the directions recorded here, not part of the first build. [Proposed] (D-022) Two others, the learning record and the bridge from high school to college, wait for a later revision of this document. [Unresolved] The chapter works as a seed bank, not a field: ideas stored so nothing is lost, and planted only when their own evidence calls for them. [Proposed]

None of these is a commitment. Each needs its own evidence, governance review, and recorded decision before any work starts. [Established] (v0.1 D-020) They are written down so good ideas aren't lost, and so none of them quietly widens the scope.

| Direction | What it would do | Why it waits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High-school-to-college bridging | Connecting prospective and incoming students with VSU before they enroll | Not yet defined. The Education Protocol Concept is a separate project, not part of CAMPUS. [Unresolved] |
| Learning record | Linking programs, courses, competencies, projects, and achievements across a student's time at VSU | Academic records are sensitive, and CAMPUS must never become the authoritative record system. [Deferred] |
| Research discovery | Finding researchers, expertise, projects, laboratories, and publications | Must not duplicate VSU's research repositories or management systems. It could grow from the network's relational model and the topics members choose to help with (D-071, D-075). [Deferred] |
| Analytics and AI, including GIS | Insight and assistance built on governed data | Only in this order: governed data, meaningful relationships, a validated use case, then analytics or AI. Never the reverse. [Deferred] |

The digital twin was listed here until 9 October 2026, when it moved out, keeping the same conditions; it now has its own chapter. [Established] ([Chapter 4](04-digital-twin.md); D-077; D-081)

Pathways, v0.1's education-to-career dimension, is not on the current list. It stays parked in the archive unless a decision revives it (v0.1 §7.4; D-022).

## What it takes to start one

A direction moves forward only with evidence in four areas at once (v0.1 §12.26):

- **Users:** people show that the capability is valuable.
- **Technology:** the architecture holds up at the next scope.
- **Institution:** VSU's environment can support it.
- **Governance:** data, privacy, security, and ownership are defined.

If any one is missing, the direction stays on this page.
