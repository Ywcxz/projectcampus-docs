# Later Directions

*Possible futures, recorded so they don't steer today's work*

None of the directions below is a commitment. Each needs its own evidence, governance review, and recorded decision before any work starts. [Established] (v0.1 D-020) They are written down so good ideas aren't lost, and so none of them quietly widens the scope: a seed bank, not a field. [Proposed]

## What it takes to start one

A direction moves forward only with evidence in four areas at once (v0.1 §12.26):

- **Users:** people show that the capability is valuable.
- **Technology:** the architecture holds up at the next scope.
- **Institution:** VSU's environment can support it.
- **Governance:** data, privacy, security, and ownership are defined.

If any one is missing, the direction stays on this page.
